# Assets and provenance

- The shuttle mark and eight league SVG assets are original SHUTTL design assets included in this repository.
- Manrope and Barlow font files are bundled locally. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included under `public/fonts` and must remain with redistributed fonts.
- Icons use `lucide-react`; its license is distributed with the dependency.
- Runtime dependencies retain their upstream licenses through npm. The Gaussian CDF uses `@stdlib/math-base-special-erfc`.
- The 400 rating fixtures contain synthetic values, independently generated from Python TrueSkill 0.4.5 with seed 81001. They contain no player identities or copied match datasets.
- The home-screen preview and demo seed use fictional player names, scores and activity. Screenshots in `docs/mockups` show those fictional samples.
- No raw external badminton match records, private player data, research downloads or local credentials are included in this source publication.
