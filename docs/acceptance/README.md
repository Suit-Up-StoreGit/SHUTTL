## Outcome measures

- **First-result activation — unmeasured.** Share of new players who get one correctly published result. Track start, submission, approvals and publication; distinguish sessions from players.

- **Five-result activation — unmeasured.** Share reaching reveal in their preferred category. Measure elapsed days and abandonment stage, not just account creation.

- **First-value time — unmeasured.** Time from joining to the first benefit the player actually recognizes. Combine task timing with user explanation of value.

- **Result-completion rate — unmeasured.** Share of submitted valid results reaching required approval. Separate ordinary, retrospective, guest and corrected results.

- **Approval latency — unmeasured.** Time until each required player responds and the result publishes. Report median and upper-tail delays; distinguish notification delivery.

- **Recording task success — unmeasured.** Share recording correct sides and scores without help. Measure errors, corrections and completion time on real phones.

- **Rating comprehension — unmeasured.** Share correctly explaining category independence and partner effects. Use concrete examples rather than agreement with a leading question.

- **Premium perception — unmeasured.** Independent target-player perception of quality. Blind comparative review of complete journeys, not one curated screenshot.

- **Voluntary return rate — unmeasured.** Useful returns without an experimenter prompt. Distinguish checking, approval, sharing and playing-associated returns.

- **Day-7 and day-30 retention — unmeasured.** Return behavior in clearly defined player cohorts. Publish the retained-activity definition and account for weekly playing cadence.

- **Eight-week meaningful retention — unmeasured.** Continued evidence, useful checking or group activity over eight weeks. Segment active-playing players, quiet weeks and category preference.

- **Post-loss retention — unmeasured.** Subsequent useful activity after a published loss. Compare like-for-like players and contexts; avoid assigning causation from raw cohorts.

- **Rivalry engagement — unmeasured.** Voluntary use of supported comparisons and lead-change information. Measure relevant rival views and returns without inventing activity.

- **Share propensity — unmeasured.** Share of genuine earned moments voluntarily shared. Separate download, copied link and confirmed downstream use.

- **Invitation activation — unmeasured.** Invited players reaching their own first useful value. Track the actual invitation chain and privacy-preserving referral context.

- **Cohort network density — unmeasured.** Useful repeated playing links inside a launch group. Measure active confirmed relationships, not friend counts alone.

- **Willingness to pay — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Actual purchase at a clearly stated price. Run real offers with representative players rather than interpreting likes as demand.

- **Paid renewal and cancellation — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Retention of paying cohorts with recorded reasons. Observe more than initial trial conversion or founder supporters.

- **Badminton predictive calibration — unmeasured.** Agreement between modeled probabilities and held-out outcomes. Evaluate Singles, Doubles and Mixed separately, including scoring formats, relevant subgroups and uncertainty.

- **Fraud and dispute burden — unmeasured.** Near-duplicates, suspicious clusters, reports and review cost. Measure actual incidence, false positives, decisions and time to resolution.

- **Customer satisfaction — unmeasured.** Player satisfaction after a complete normal session. Collect CSAT alongside the reason, outcome and device; avoid founder-led prompting.

- **Customer effort — unmeasured.** Perceived effort of scoring, approving and finding useful information. Use a short CES question after actual tasks and compare with measured errors and time.

- **Recommendation intent — unmeasured.** Likelihood of recommending SHUTTL to another relevant player. Collect NPS with reasons and invitation behavior; NPS alone does not establish growth.

- **Product-market-fit sentiment — unmeasured.** Response to losing access among players who experienced core value. Segment established active players and record what benefit would be missed.

- **DAU, WAU and MAU — unmeasured.** Unique meaningful active players within daily, weekly and monthly windows. Define activity explicitly; compare ratios with the cohort's real playing cadence.

- **Paid churn — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Share of paying members or revenue lost in a stated period. Separate voluntary cancellation, failed payment, downgrade and ordinary non-playing weeks.

- **Trial conversion — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Share of appropriately timed trials becoming genuinely paid subscriptions. Observe actual charges, activation and later renewal; do not count card entry as conversion.

- **Refund and payment failure — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Billing failures, refunds and recovery of legitimate subscription access. Track cause, time to resolve, player communication and accidental access loss.

- **Revenue per user — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Period revenue per active user and per paying user. Use matching periods and denominators; distinguish gross billings, net revenue and refunds.

- **Customer acquisition cost — unmeasured.** Attributable acquisition cost per newly activated or paying player. Include creator campaign costs and distinguish channels; views are not new customers.

- **Cohort lifetime contribution — unmeasured.** Observed or explicitly modeled net contribution from a player cohort. Include retention uncertainty and support, review and service costs; avoid an unsupported lifetime forecast.

- **Contribution margin — commercial measurement deferred; billing excluded.** Revenue remaining after relevant variable delivery and support costs. Measure payment fees, hosting, review, support and any paid content or coaching delivery.

- **Referral conversion — unmeasured.** Invitations per activated player and activation of unique recipients. Identify actual referral chains without double-counting an existing group's overlapping invitations.

- **Creator funnel conversion — unmeasured.** View, click, visit, signup, first result, reveal, payment and renewal progression. Use clear channel attribution and unique-player denominators; combined followers may overlap.

- **Experience latency — unmeasured.** Loading, interaction, submission, approval and publication latency. Measure median and high-percentile behavior on ordinary devices and real networks at launch load.

- **Crash-free journeys — unmeasured.** Share of core sessions without an unrecovered application failure. Track frontend failures and backend errors by device, action and release.

- **Accessibility task success — unmeasured.** Unassisted task success with relevant assistive technology and enlarged text. Run complete scoring, approval, sharing and account-control journeys on target devices.

- **Support resolution — unmeasured.** Time and quality of account, scoring, consent and dispute support. Track first response, resolution, reopening and satisfaction without assuming every report is dishonest.

- **Comparison fairness — unmeasured.** Predictive and experience quality across play categories and relevant cohorts. Test network density, uncertainty, partner patterns and skill ranges without unjustified claims of equivalence.

- **Data recovery quality — unmeasured.** Restore time, integrity and recoverable loss after a simulated failure. Perform a separate restore drill and reconcile approved ledger, identities and published ratings.
