# SHUTTL player evidence and acceptance protocol

The acceptance bar remains 9.5 for every in-scope criterion. The application is a pilot, and the [150-criterion register](acceptance/README.md) is not a certificate. Payment integration is excluded. No estimated retention, invented tester quote, projected conversion or self-issued 9.5 is admissible evidence.

## Establish the study before collecting answers

Freeze the tested commit, device/browser list, launch geography, intended audience, comparison product versions, research questions, task wording, metric definitions and acceptance method before recruitment. Store dated decisions next to the results. A researcher who did not implement the interface should moderate; reviewers should not be rewarded for high scores. Founder friends and followers can supply useful findings, but must be labeled separately from less-engaged target players. Record incomplete and failed journeys as well as successes.

The suggested first round is 12–18 adult players, including recreational, competitive, singles-oriented, doubles-oriented, Mixed, infrequent players, different genders, different phone budgets and relevant accessibility needs. These are discovery-planning numbers, not a sufficient sample for every numerical acceptance claim. Follow with an eight-week observational pilot in several existing badminton groups. A starting recruitment plan of 6 groups of 8–12 consenting players is practical for studying network effects; it does not establish population retention or creator-scale demand. Determine quantitative sample sizes from the uncertainty and effect size required, before running those tests.

Choose the initial market explicitly. The present interface is English and district pools use Singapore names. Players elsewhere can use rating/friend workflows, but that is not equivalent to complete global local-status support. An international release needs a location taxonomy, localized text and relevant support ownership. Youth participation is outside the adult pilot.

Recruitment and research invitations are prepared here for the operator to use. This repository work sends no messages to players, followers or external services. Ask for informed research consent, separately from rated-play consent. Offer withdrawal and explain which pseudonymous match references may remain necessary for other participants' ledger. Avoid collecting precise location, contact lists or private conversations. Keep raw notes and identity keys in restricted research storage, outside Git.

## A strict, non-inflated scoring rule

For each criterion, record the original acceptance statement, current build, observed evidence, evidence limitations, independent reviewer rationale, score and unresolved blockers. Use the original 1–10 quality scale consistently; 9.5 means independently demonstrated excellence on that specific criterion, with no material known defect in its intended use. It must not mean “this feature exists,” “the tests passed,” “95% by arithmetic,” or “the founder likes it.”

Ask two independent reviewers to judge every applicable criterion using the same evidence. Retain both scores and reasons. If either reviewer scores below 9.5, or evidence is incomplete, the criterion stays unaccepted. Do not average a low score away. For subjective criteria, also retain representative target-player distributions and critical comments; a favorable mean must not hide a failing subgroup or device. For numerical outcome criteria, define the actual target, denominator, time window, confidence method and planned sample before collection. There is no universal transformation from retention, conversion, predictive accuracy or latency into a quality score of 9.5. Have an independent statistical reviewer approve that mapping if the product owner insists on a common scale.

Missing evidence is recorded as **unmeasured**, not zero, 9.5 or a theoretical guarantee. A finding can justify a design change without proving an outcome. Exclusions need an explicit scope decision; they cannot silently disappear from the register. Criterion 138 is excluded because payment integration was explicitly deferred. Price/value criteria remain unknown despite that exclusion.

Acceptance needs three distinct records: implemented behavior, target-player evidence and operational readiness. Passing the first does not pass the other two. Keep the historical earlier-build scores separate from new evidence. Fix and retest a failing criterion before revising its score. Freeze a final release candidate and repeat changed journeys; do not recycle results from a different build without assessing applicability.

## Unassisted usability tasks

Give players goals in ordinary language. Do not name the button or supply the answer. Start with an empty account; include developed histories and pending/corrected results later. Use distinct fictional fixtures for consent/error tasks so no player's real rating is altered by research mistakes. Normal observational play must use real consenting participants and real completed matches.

| Task                                     | Observe and record                                                              | Failure that blocks acceptance                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Explain SHUTTL after the first screen    | Own words about what a rating does and does not establish                       | Describes certified skill, guaranteed wins or a universal neighbourhood rank                          |
| Join and recover after changing phones   | Unassisted completion, recovery-code understanding, elapsed time, help required | Cannot regain access or exposes recovery code to another player                                       |
| Find the normal playing category         | Category selection, saved preference and independent placement counts           | Assumes Doubles results update Singles/Mixed                                                          |
| Add a regular player                     | Discovery success, invitation understanding and recipient response              | Assumes a friend request grants rated-play consent                                                    |
| Agree before a session                   | Exact roster/category/format and personal opt-in teach-back                     | Believes one organizer can consent for everyone                                                       |
| Record Singles and Doubles               | Correct sides, score/format/time, player identity, task time and errors         | Wrong roster, wrong score, accidental duplicate or unchecked change after acknowledgement             |
| Record the same lineup again             | Roster reuse time and fresh score/time review                                   | Reused old score/time or silently carries acknowledgement                                             |
| Approve the exact score                  | Required players, revision ID and score understanding                           | A teammate replaces an eligible opponent or silence becomes approval                                  |
| Handle a live correction                 | Read changed score, clear acknowledgement, fresh review                         | A prior checkbox approves the replacement revision                                                    |
| Correct and dispute a published result   | Published effect, waiting state, reviewer process                               | Believes a dispute automatically deletes a legitimate loss                                            |
| Reveal the fifth category result         | Starting estimate, uncertainty, hidden-prior delta, badge meaning               | Interprets five results as certification or placement as a visible rating loss                        |
| Compare with a friend                    | Supported gap, tie, pair-linked and disconnected states                         | Ranks disconnected estimates as reliable or assigns teammates' individual contribution from team wins |
| Inspect progress and a quiet/loss week   | Point/date/value, period coverage, peak, actual wins                            | Confuses activity count with skill or inactivity with punitive mean decay                             |
| Find settings, goals and an older result | Navigation, filters, pagination, back/reload                                    | A task is blocked by accumulated history or a mislabeled destination                                  |
| Share a real milestone privately         | Name/district choices, historical date, PNG versus hosted revocation            | Accidentally exposes identity or assumes downloaded copies can be recalled                            |
| Configure and revoke device alerts       | Explicit permission, channel choices, quiet hours, logout                       | Unwanted opt-in, leaked lock-screen names/ratings, or another account receives old device alerts      |
| Sign out, block, export and delete       | Mobile access, account separation and disclosure                                | Old private account material remains accessible to the next user                                      |

For each task keep: anonymous participant key, tested commit, device/browser, text-size/motion settings, task start/end, correct completion, errors, recovery/help, severity, player explanation and researcher notes. Record the actual interface state. Do not infer understanding from a checked box. Repeat with keyboard-only, VoiceOver/TalkBack/NVDA as appropriate, 200% text, reduced motion, intermittent connection and a small phone. Chrome width emulation is useful evidence but does not substitute for iOS/Android hardware.

## Design, premium feel and rewards

Review complete onboarding, ordinary home, placement, reveal, winning, losing, promotion, personal-best, correction, comparison, profile, settings, sharing and error journeys. Show real export files. Include long names, sparse networks, no recent activity, an old result, high/low league names and ordinary phone performance. Choose one default direction after testing; five concepts are alternatives, not a reason to count five independently accepted experiences.

Ask for independent 1–10 judgments on recognizable identity, badminton relevance, quality of typography, hierarchy, legibility, restraint, sporting aspiration, motion, badge desirability, personal relevance, confidence in the number, and willingness to share. First ask what feels confusing, unnecessary, cheap, generic or unearned. Then ask for a score with its reason. Randomize concept order and obscure the founder preference. Where a comparison is used, record the actual current product/version and comparable task; do not call an app “Apple/Nike/Strava quality” solely from brand associations.

Observe whether players voluntarily revisit a result, inspect a rival, use a historical point or share a legitimate milestone. Check celebration fatigue across repeated wins and losses. A short animation or desirable badge does not establish habit. Do not add fake activity, countdowns, ratings for checking in or unvalidated crowns to make FOMO look stronger.

Use creator-approved original images or educational material only when supplied/licensed and demonstrably useful. A founding attribution exists; a substantive Featherballer content offering has not been created. Likewise, refined initials do not demonstrate friend-photo recognition. These gaps remain explicit in the register.

## Retention and the social loop

Measure useful actions separately: recording, reviewing, approved publication, inspecting a supported comparison, viewing real activity/history, voluntarily sharing and a return tied to actual play. Define what counts as a meaningful return before collection. Track invitation receipt, signup, first correctly published result and fifth-result reveal as separate stages. Never use total views/followers as active player counts; overlapping followers and campaign visits need appropriate denominators.

The supplied `npm run pilot:report` reads an **existing database read-only** and prints aggregate ledger proxies. It includes registered accounts, including deleted accounts to reduce survivor bias. A ledger return means submitting an original result or approving a revision. It cannot measure opening the app, understanding the rating, inspecting a rival, voluntary share use, support quality or purchase intent. A first-publication timestamp measures availability of a result, not recognition of user value. Demo databases are explicitly flagged. Verify actual cohort membership separately; this is not an analytics or experiment-assignment platform.

The report's Day-7 window is days 7–13 after account creation, and Day-30 is days 30–36. Only players with the full window observed enter the denominator. Use submission/approval timestamps, never backdated playing dates, for a return. An immature cohort returns null for its rate; do not report it as failed retention. These windows are a declared study convention, not an industry benchmark. A daily-visit or exact-day definition would be a different measure and must be labeled separately.

Use voluntary consented research diaries or a separately approved privacy-preserving event plan for comparison/history/checking returns. The current build does not collect those view events. Do not bolt covert tracking onto the pilot. Record playing cadence, category, cohort, prior engagement and quiet weeks. Retain churned participants in analysis. Compare post-loss activity among otherwise comparable players; raw correlations do not establish that reward copy caused retention.

For network effects, measure genuine repeated confirmed playing edges, reciprocal accepted relationships, supported comparison coverage and useful group participation. Large friend counts alone do not establish a dense playing network. Trial a voluntary weekly group ritual using actual results; the application currently offers an individual recap and does not supply an automatic circle digest. Record actual recipient value before adding notification volume.

At weeks 1, 2, 4 and 8 ask what benefit the player would miss, what they ignored, when they last returned voluntarily and what made them leave. Distinguish satisfaction, customer effort, recommendation intent and real invitations. Keep distributions and reasons; no single sentiment score proves product-market fit.

## Badminton rating calibration

The shipped method is an uncertainty-aware Gaussian team model, with independent category ledgers and exact comparison-evidence checks. Arithmetic reference tests verify implementation. They do not prove that the candidate beta/tau/prior values, five-result reveal, league thresholds or title policy are best for badminton.

Collect consented real Singles, Doubles and Mixed outcomes with exact team identities, score format, played time, revisions, category, approval provenance and naturally varying partnerships. Keep identity keys restricted; use stable pseudonymous IDs in the analysis. Do not use demo wins or retrospectively invented matches as calibration data. Avoid asking people to change normal partnerships just to manufacture a desired matrix.

Before tuning, reserve chronological held-out periods and at least some independent playing networks. Fit/tune on training data only; later matches, corrected future outcomes and the same final ledger must not leak into earlier predictions. Predict each held-out match using only information available at that point, then compare with its subsequently confirmed outcome. Publish missing-data, guest-claim, excluded/disputed-result and format policies.

Compare suitable candidate team models and a simpler Elo-type baseline. Use held-out proper probability scores (log loss and Brier), reliability/calibration plots with uncertainty, ranking usefulness for the actual group task, cold-start convergence, fixed-pair versus rotated-partner cases, disconnected networks and category/format subgroups. Inspect surprising losses and high-confidence mistakes. Do not optimize only win prediction while ignoring comparability and user explanation. No leaderboard position can repair unidentifiable individual evidence.

Evaluate dynamic uncertainty on genuinely changing skills and long breaks. Compare alternative reveal rules against convergence, comprehensibility and abandonment, without exposing hidden provisional means as losses. Validate league spacing and desirability separately from prediction. A percentile/club-level claim needs an appropriate representative population; the current league names do not make such a claim.

Freeze model versions and independently reproduce replay. Test changes on copies and explain any rating-history migration to players. Local titles remain disabled by default. Enable them only after an approved eligibility/evidence policy, eligible-pool checks, calibrated uncertainty and abuse review. Do not reinterpret a developing number as a certified neighbourhood champion.

## Manipulation, privacy and support

Exercise duplicate submissions, time-shifted near duplicates, reciprocal false wins, isolated colluding clusters, account rotation, claimed guest aliases, forged approval actors, stale revisions, blocked users, foreign history cursors, foreign share/device IDs and self-adjudication. Record attack cost, observability, review burden, false positives and appeal outcomes. Current consent/integrity controls are useful; they do not prove a physical match occurred or implement a mature collusion classifier.

Appoint a human reviewer/support owner before the observational pilot. Publish a truthful response target suited to actual staffing. Describe evidence access, recusal, appeal, queue escalation, player updates, backup operator and incident ownership. The interface does not promise an invented support SLA. Measure first response, resolution, reopening and satisfaction. Delayed response records remain private and factual; playing ratings must not become punishment for notification failures.

Review launch-market privacy, age and consumer requirements with appropriate qualified help before public rollout. The code provides choices and controls; it does not provision a legal/operational service. Verify retention policies and deletion/export behavior on the hosted system. Downloaded snapshots are external copies; hosted revocation cannot recall them.

## Real devices, hosting and release gates

Configure a private HTTPS staging host, persistent database and real operator-owned VAPID identity. Test notification opt-in, denial, quiet hours, revoked/stale subscription, logout/login to another account, recovery, deletion, break mode, background delivery, click continuation and cleared old notifications. On iOS use a Home Screen web app. Provider acceptance is not verified device delivery. Use consenting testers and actual devices; supplied worker tests use stub senders and contact no providers.

Load-test expected creator traffic in the intended environment: browsing, signup, lookups, submissions, concurrent approvals, historical corrections, replay and sharing. State concurrency, arrival rate, ledger size, network graph, hardware, rate-limit behavior and p50/p95/p99 latency. Confirm responsiveness while categories replay. Current SQLite/synchronous replay and bounded exact evidence are single-instance pilot limits. Do not extrapolate a small local demo timing to hundreds of thousands of viewers. A multi-worker/distributed design needs separate engineering and evidence before that rollout.

Run a hosted backup/restore drill, interruption during publication, repeated replay, private data retention checks and alerts routed to a real responsible operator. Verify health monitoring and player communications. Run the supplied Docker configuration in the target environment before calling it verified. GitHub push is not deployment, and a local test is not a production recovery drill.

## Price and offer

No payment integration or paid entitlement is introduced in this pass. Users can experience the current loop without a paywall. That does not establish a broad $20 monthly offer. Keep willingness to pay, renewal, quiet-month value, recurring guidance and organizer economics **unmeasured**. Research existing value first. Later, test a concrete truthful offer with representative users and actual purchase/renewal evidence. Likes, creator reach, founder supporters and a “would you pay?” answer are weaker evidence and must be labeled accordingly.

If coaching/content is proposed, use creator-approved observations or legitimate expertise; win/loss outcomes alone cannot diagnose stroke mechanics. If an organizer plan is proposed, measure actual screening/time savings in existing groups. Do not add hosting, tournaments or cosmetics to dilute the rating-first V1 scope.

## Results record and next iteration

For every study round, save the frozen protocol, tested commit, recruitment provenance, anonymized task observations, raw distributions, known limitations, independent scores, criterion IDs affected, required fixes and retest plan. Keep an explicit link from each claim to evidence. Maintain a separate incident/critical-defect list; high aesthetic scores never waive consent/privacy/data-integrity defects.

The loop is: collect evidence → inspect failures → implement justified fixes → retest changed tasks → independently reassess that criterion. Broad acceptance remains closed until every in-scope criterion actually meets its declared bar. The supplied register starts with zero independently accepted criteria because that evidence has not yet been collected.
